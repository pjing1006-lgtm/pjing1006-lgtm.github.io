About
me

I'm a designer from Nanchang, China, now based in Vancouver.

I studied Art and Design at the University of Alberta, and I'm now in the MDes program at Emily Carr University.

I'm interested in the things that have always been part of everyday life, but that we've learned not to see. Many of them sit just outside the edges of the systems we live by: what a standard leaves out, what a diagnosis misses, what a routine quietly decides for us.

My work tries to bring those edges into view. I make things people can wear, touch, play or step into, so they can feel how something they usually overlook is shaping them, and the lives around them.

“To question that which seems to have ceased forever to astonish us.”

— Georges Perec, Approaches to What?
